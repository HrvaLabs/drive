_Last updated: 2026-06-11_

HrvaDrive is a **free, non-commercial hobby project** that turns an OBD2 drive log (exported from the
Car Scanner app) into a drive report. By using it you agree to these terms. **This document is a
template for whoever operates an instance of HrvaDrive; if you self-host or deploy it, you are
responsible for your own terms.** It is not legal advice.

## The service

- Free to use, **no account**, no payment, no sign-up.
- The analysis server is **stateless** — your upload is processed in memory and returned; your saved
  history lives **only in your browser**. See the [Privacy Notice](PRIVACY.md).
- The service may change, break, or be discontinued **at any time without notice**. Keep a backup of
  your history (the in-app **Download backup** button) if it matters to you.

## Your use

- Upload only drive logs **you are entitled to use**. A log can contain personal data (VIN, GPS) —
  uploading data about someone else is your responsibility.
- Don't abuse the service: no automated bulk traffic beyond personal use, no attempts to disrupt it,
  no use that is unlawful where you are.

## Estimates, not advice

The reports — fuel consumption, costs, CO₂, eco scores, engine-health hints, maintenance flags — are
**estimates derived from your log**. Sensor data can be wrong, incomplete, or misread. Nothing in a
report is professional automotive, financial, or safety advice. **Verify with a mechanic before
acting on a health or maintenance hint, and never read a report while driving.**
