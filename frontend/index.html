HRVADRIVE · OBD2 REPORTS

Understand your drive, not just the dashboard.

HrvaDrive turns Car Scanner ELM OBD2 CSV exports into reports with route maps, fuel economy and cost estimates, eco-driving tips, and engine insights. It is free and does not require an account.

Your driving history stays in your browser. Uploaded logs are processed in memory by a stateless analysis service and are not stored by the application.

Created by Karlo Hrvačić as a HrvaLabs project. Source code.
